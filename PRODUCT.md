# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating Shanvit — recruiters, potential collaborators, other builders. Arriving from a cold link (Twitter, LinkedIn, a referral). Typically a single visit; skimming to answer "who is this, what are they building, should I reach out?"

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Shanvit S Shetty — a full-stack engineer moving deeper into ML, LLMs, and agentic systems. The site's job is to make someone credible and worth reaching out to in under 90 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Terse. Builder.
Voice cue: "chaos. code. control." — three words, period after each. Not soft, not decorative. Things earn their place or get cut.

## Anti-references

Not inferred; user skipped. Default: avoid SaaS-cream minimalism, pastel AI startup aesthetics, and résumé-with-a-gradient.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn every element.** If it doesn't communicate something, remove it.
2. **Current beats comprehensive.** Show what's alive now; /projects is the archive.
3. **Let the work speak.** Copy describes what was built, not how impressive it was.
4. **Terse is confident.** Short labels, tight descriptions, no filler.
5. **Clarity first, then craft.** Motion and polish serve understanding; they don't substitute for it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support required on all animations.
